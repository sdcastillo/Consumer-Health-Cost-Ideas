    <h2 id="idea-title">See what other people paid, and what happened</h2>
    <p>This note sketches a data product for people who need treatment and want to pay less for it. Chronic-condition communities already do a version of the work in public. Someone describes a diagnosis, and other people answer with what they tried, what it cost, and how it turned out. The idea is to make that exchange a dataset instead of a thread.</p>
    <p>The loop is three steps. A person uses HIPAA to get their own medical records, uploads those records to a database, and then can read the other records in the pool. The fields that matter are the ones a patient can label: the outcome they actually had, and the cost. A chart without those two labels does not answer the question the product is for.</p>
    <p>It is for patients with a chronic condition who already compare notes, and for anyone sketching a health-cost product in that shape. There is no app here, no schema, and no privacy review. On the SamWiki ladder this is <strong>Level 1 · Beginner</strong>: a first look you can read in a few minutes.</p>

## The idea, as written

Help people with chronic conditions see what others do. The product is a data-driven version of what Reddit’s chronic-condition communities already do.

1. A person uses HIPAA to get their own medical records.
2. They upload those records to a database.
3. They gain access to the other records: medical outcomes labeled by users, and **cost**.
